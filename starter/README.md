# Coach starter kit

Your own AI running and cycling coach, built and run by Claude:

- a training plan written for you, kept in a Claude doc;
- the **Coach app**: today's workout, a week calendar with TrainingPeaks-style compliance colours, a daily check-in, RPE after every workout, and fitness/fatigue/form charts;
- a **weekly coaching update** every Sunday. It reads your watch data and check-ins, adjusts next week, loads the workouts onto your watch, and sends you a report.

## How to start (about 30 minutes)

1. Open **Claude** (the desktop app works best if you want watch sync) and start a new conversation.
2. Paste this message:

   > Set me up with my own coach using the starter kit at https://github.com/fonsi43/fonsi-coach/tree/main/starter. Read SETUP.md there first and follow it.

3. Answer Claude's questions about you, your races and your training.
4. Claude builds your plan and your app, then sets up the weekly update.

**What you need**
- A Claude plan with scheduled tasks and artifacts.
- **For automatic watch sync:** a Garmin watch, plus a computer with the Claude desktop app linked to the conversation. Claude helps you install the Garmin connection.
- **Without Garmin:** it still works from your check-ins and the workouts you tick off in the app.

Your data stays in your own Claude account. Nothing is shared with the person who sent you this.
