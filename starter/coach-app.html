Fitness, fatigue and form

Fitness (6-week load)Fatigue (7-day load)Form = fitness − fatigue

Every session earns training-load points based on how long and how hard it was. Fitness rises slowly as you train consistently; fatigue rises fast after hard days and drops fast with rest. Form below 0 means you are building (tired but getting fitter); 5–25 before a race means fresh and ready.

How much of your running was easy

Of all the minutes you ran each week, how many were truly easy (heart rate under about 152)?

Good marathon training is about 75–80% easy (the green band). Easy running builds the engine; too many medium-hard runs leave you tired without the benefit. Before Chicago you were often near 20–30%.
